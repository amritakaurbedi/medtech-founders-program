How to Enter

Four steps to compete

1

Form your team

Teams of 1–5 students. You can enter alone, but we’d encourage bringing a team — the projects are stronger for it.

2

Submit your RSVP

Register your team members and choose your track. MedTech Founders Program participants enter free; all other participants pay an $8 entry fee.

3

Build your pitch deck

Prepare a deck covering your problem, solution, feasibility, and market. A sample deck and the judging rubric are available in advance.

4

Pitch on the day

Five-minute presentation followed by two minutes of Q&A from the judges. Winners are announced at the awards ceremony.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can participate?

Students of all years, majors, and skill levels. No entrepreneurship experience is required. This year Open MIC is open to students from other universities, not just UCI.

How large can my team be?

Teams can have 1–5 students. Working alone is allowed, but we encourage teaming up.

Is there an entry fee?

MedTech Founders Program participants compete free. All other participants pay $8. Spectators attend free.

How are teams judged?

A panel of judges scores each pitch against a published rubric, which is shared in advance so you know exactly what you’re being evaluated on. Winners are announced at the awards ceremony.

Can I come just to watch?

Yes. The pitch session is free and open to spectators — a good way to see what the competition involves before entering next year.

What if I don’t have an idea yet?

Come to the workshops. We run sessions on ideation, entrepreneurship, and pitching ahead of the competition, plus office hours where you can talk through a concept with coordinators.
