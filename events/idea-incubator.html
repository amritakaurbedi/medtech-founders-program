Idea Incubator

Present a research poster on the clinical problem you’ve identified and the solution you’re proposing — and get real feedback before you start building.

DateTue, Nov 24, 2026
TimeTBA

FormatPoster presentation
See it on the calendar Poster template & samples

What happens

Idea Incubator closes out the Fall ideation phase. Each team presents a poster and talks through their thinking with attendees who circulate the room.

  • Present your poster. Lay out the problem, your proposed solution, technical feasibility, and next steps.
  • Talk to judges one-on-one. Professors, industry guests, and NVC mentors move between posters asking questions.
  • Collect written feedback. Attendees write down comments, which you’ll use to sharpen your concept going into Winter.
  • See what other teams are doing. One of the better parts — you’ll leave with ideas you didn’t walk in with.
How to Prepare

What to have ready

1

Complete your design reviews

CODR and PDR come first. The feedback from those reviews should shape what ends up on your poster.

2

Build your poster

Start from the template on the resources page. A full sample poster is there too, showing the expected depth and structure.

3

Submit and print

Posters are due ahead of the event so there’s time to print. Check the calendar for the deadline — printing takes longer than you think.

4

Practice your walkthrough

You’ll explain your project dozens of times over the session. Have a tight two-minute version ready.
